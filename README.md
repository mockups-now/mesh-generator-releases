# Mesh Generator for Adobe Photoshop

## Turn Your Photos Into Editable Smart Object Mockups

Creating realistic mockups shouldn't require hours of manually adjusting perspective and warping layers.

Mesh Generator is a Photoshop plugin that analyzes the shape of a surface in your photo and creates a transformed, editable Smart Object that follows its geometry.

Made for surface pattern designers, professional mockup creators, graphic designers, and online sellers showcasing artwork on fabric, clothing, home decor, and other products.

## From Photo to Smart Object in a Few Clicks

### Step 1. Select Your Surface

Open your image in Photoshop and select the area where you want your design to appear.

Use a supported Photoshop selection method, such as the Lasso or Quick Selection tool, or a selection created from a layer mask.

![Cozy corner with the blanket selected using the layer mask](https://mockups.now/images/mesh/cozy_corner_original.jpg)

### Step 2. Click the Magic Button

Open Mesh Generator and click **Generate**.

The plugin analyzes the image using computer vision and mathematical fitting algorithms to estimate the geometry of the selected surface.

No manual mesh construction. No painstakingly adjusting dozens of control points.

![Mesh Generator plugin view with the "Generate from selection" button active](https://mockups.now/images/mesh/the_magic_button.png)

### Step 3. Add Your Design

After a few seconds, you'll have a new Smart Object layer with a mesh transformation applied.

![Cozy corner with the default grid applied](https://mockups.now/images/mesh/cozy_corner_default_pattern.jpg)

Open the embedded Smart Object, insert your pattern, texture, or image, and save. Your artwork follows the estimated geometry of the original surface, including curves, perspective, and folds.

![Cozy corner with the smart object mesh view](https://mockups.now/images/mesh/cozy_corner_mesh.jpg)

**And the best part?** You can reuse the generated Smart Object with as many different designs as you like without spending another generation.

![Cozy corner with a custom seamless pattern applied (pattern by katesu.design)](https://mockups.now/images/mesh/cozy_corner_custom.jpg)
